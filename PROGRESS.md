# Progress

**Current phase: 5 of 6 — Use it**

## Where things stand
Rebuilt as a single readable `index.html`. Athletes pick their event (100, 200,
400) and get each day's workout, matched to the time of year. Meets can be
added to any day and the week rearranges around them. Works offline once
opened.

### Done
- Event picker; plan for 100 / 200 / 400 across five times of year
- Hard/easy weeks, never two hard days in a row; weight room on Mon and Wed
- Meet days with automatic pre-meet and recovery days
- Tap-to-tick steps, "how to" for every exercise, time boxes with best times
- Race plan with splits from a goal time
- Sources listed in the app
- Wenatchee purple and gold, school logo, home-screen install, offline
